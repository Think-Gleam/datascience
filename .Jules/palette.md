## Palette's Journal
## 2026-03-20 - Found missing ARIA labels on icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (using `size="icon"`) in the app lacking proper `aria-label` attributes, affecting accessibility for screen reader users. Specifically, the theme toggle, mobile menu, close menu, go back to course, edit course, and delete course buttons.
**Action:** Adding `aria-label` attributes to all icon-only buttons for better a11y, fulfilling the micro-UX goal.
